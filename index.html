THE ATELIER PROTOCOL

4 STAGES OF
OBSESSIVE PRECISION.

Our proprietary protocol ensures that no blade touches vehicle metal and every edge is meticulously wrapped around panel boundaries.

PHASE 01 / PURIFICATION

SPECTRAL SURFACE CORRECTION

Multi-stage fallout bath, non-abrasive clay bar, and single-micron jewel rotary correction eliminating factory orange peel and wash scratches.

MEASUREMENT: ELCOMETER 456 ULTRASONIC GAUGE
PHASE 02 / DIGITIZATION

CAD LASER TEMPLATE PLOTTING

Every panel is plotted in-house with extended margins for seamless edge tucking. Zero knives, scalpels, or blades touch painted factory surfaces.

SOFTWARE: 3D SCANNER ATELIER SUITE
PHASE 03 / INSTALLATION

CLASS-1000 CLEAN ROOM APPLICATION

Installed in our positive-pressure, HEPA-filtered clean room. Optical slip solution ensures zero airborne dust contamination underneath the film.

ENVIRONMENT: POSITIVE PRESSURE HEPA H14
PHASE 04 / ENCAPSULATION

INFRARED ANNEALING & CERAMIC BOND

Short-wave infrared lamps lock thermoplastic memory at 90°C before applying molecular SiO2 ceramic coating for permanent hydrophobic repellency.

CURING: SHORTWAVE INFRARED RADIANT LAMPS
